# Issue 1: Money sums are not rounded

- Category: `bug`
- State: `ready-for-agent`
- Severity: must fix (Ponytail audit v1.0.6, finding 1)

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Problem

Invoice totals, paid amounts, and pay-run totals are added with plain
JavaScript addition, which cannot represent most decimals exactly. A EUR
invoice with charges of 1.10 + 2.20 stores a total of 3.3000000000000003, so
one payment of 3.30 leaves the invoice at Partial forever: the remaining
amount is 4.4e-16 and every further payment is refused. The same dust blocks
restoring a trashed payment (1.10 + 2.20 reads as more than 3.30), can make a
valid backup fail validation (the validator re-sums payments in a different
order and gets 6.6000000000000005 instead of 6.6), and stores a pay-run total
of 0.8699999999999999 for three sessions at 0.29. IDR is unaffected (whole
rupiah); every money test uses whole IDR amounts, which is why this survived
319 passing tests.

## Fix

One rounding helper (round to the current currency's fraction digits) applied
at every money-total write: invoice create total, payment paid amount, pay-run
total, and currency-switch totals. A tolerance (rounded comparison) in the
trash restore overpay check, the backup validator's paid-amount and total
checks, and the integrity checker's paid mismatch check. Also check whether
the fix un-sticks an already-dusty stored invoice, for example via tolerance
in status derivation; if a small change does it, include it.

## Acceptance

- EUR/USD: charges 1.10 + 2.20 paid in full reach Paid.
- Deleting then restoring a payment round-trips.
- Backup validation passes with payments in shuffled order.
- Pay-run total for three sessions at 0.29 stores exactly 0.87.
- All existing tests still pass.
