# Issue 2: Currency switch does not re-derive invoice status

- Category: `bug`
- State: `ready-for-agent`
- Severity: must fix (Ponytail audit v1.0.6, finding 2)

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Problem

Switching currency converts every payment, charge, and total, then writes the
invoice back with its old status. It never re-derives the status from the
converted amounts. Verified at rate 0.0000615: an IDR invoice of 100,000 with
two payments of 50,000 converts to paid 6.16 against a total of 6.15, so paid
exceeds total and the status is stale. The reverse case is worse: three
payments of 33,333 convert to 6.1499999999999995 against a total of 6.15, and
the invoice stays Partial with about 0 remaining and no payment able to close
it.

## Fix

After converting, re-derive the status from the converted amounts, and clamp
the paid amount to the total when the overage is rounding dust (that clamp
belongs to the rounding helper from issue 1).

## Acceptance

- A fully paid IDR invoice switched to EUR shows Paid with paid equal to total.
- A partially paid IDR invoice switched to EUR keeps a correct Partial status
  with a payable remainder.
- The reverse direction (EUR to IDR) behaves the same way.
- All existing tests still pass.
