# Issue 8: Trash snapshots keep their pre-switch currency

- Category: `bug`
- State: `ready-for-agent`
- Severity: should fix (follow-up to the v1.0.7 currency-switch fixes)

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Problem

Switching currency converts every live amount, but a trashed payment, a
trashed invoice (with its line items, paid amount, and related payments), a
trashed pay run, a trashed course, or a trashed trainer keeps its amounts in
the old currency. Restoring one after a switch adds old-currency money to a
new-currency invoice: a payment trashed at 100,000 IDR restored after a
switch to EUR comes back as 100,000 EUR. The trash is also invisible to the
check that decides whether any amount-bearing record exists, so a switch
with only trashed money takes the no-conversion path and converts nothing.

## Fix

Convert the money amounts inside live trash entries during the currency
switch, in the same transaction as the other conversions, covering every
money-bearing trash kind. Trashed money must count as amount-bearing data,
so a switch with only trashed money still fetches a rate and converts.

## Acceptance

- Switching currency with a trashed payment present, then restoring it,
  leaves the restored payment and the invoice totals in the new currency
  and consistent with each other.
- The same holds for a trashed invoice with line items and payments, and
  for trashed pay runs, courses, and trainers.
- Restoring a trashed record after a switch keeps its restore conflicts and
  audit entries working as before.
- A switch with only trashed money still converts it instead of taking the
  no-conversion path.
- All existing tests still pass.
