# Issue 7: The timezone picker opens at the top of its list, not at the selected zone

- Category: `bug`
- State: `ready-for-agent`
- Severity: should fix (follow-up to the v1.0.7 Local timezone setting)

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

## Problem

The Local timezone picker lists 419 zones. After picking Asia/Jakarta,
reopening the picker shows the top of the list (Africa/Abidjan) instead of
the selected zone: the user has to scroll through hundreds of entries to
find where they are. The cause is in the shared dropdown, not the section:
the dropdown uses Radix Select with `position="popper"`, and in that mode
Radix never runs its scroll-to-selected behaviour, while nothing caps the
menu height, so the menu renders about 15,000px tall and the selected zone
sits far below the fold, unreachable by scrolling.

## Fix

Make the menu scroll to the selected option on open, as an opt-in on the
shared dropdown used only where a long list needs it, without changing
behaviour for the other dropdowns that share it. The menu height must be
capped to the space available so the list can scroll inside the menu at all:
Radix already scrolls the selected option into view once the menu is
placed, but the scroll has no effect while the menu renders at its full
natural height with nothing to scroll.

## Acceptance

- Reopening the timezone picker shows Asia/Jakarta in view, at desktop width
  and at 390px.
- The other dropdowns that share the component behave exactly as before.
- A test asserts the menu is capped when the opt-in is on and uncapped
  without it, through the real section and the shared dropdown.
- Browser checks cover the open picker at desktop width and at 390px.
